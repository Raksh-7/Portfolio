Method

How I work in a regulated codebase

01

Reproduce and isolate

Find the smallest case that shows the fault before touching any code or opening a change record.

02

Trace it to the root cause

Step through logic and runtime behavior in Qt/C++ until the symptom points to the actual fault — not just the closest workaround.

03

Fix it under change control

Raise the change control, apply the smallest fix that resolves it, and keep the documentation trail intact as I go.

04

Verify against the standard, then close

Re-test under the original failing condition and confirm the fix holds up against IEC 62304 and EU MDR expectations before closing the record.
